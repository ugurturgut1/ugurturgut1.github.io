~/about

About

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at the University of Manchester, graduating in July 2027. I'm mainly interested in security, from hardware to software, especially digital forensics and blue teaming, along with software engineering and AI/LLM security. More generally I enjoy most things computer science, including the maths behind it.

Since last summer I've been working with the University of Liverpool on a GDPR-style evaluation of a digital health platform, for a research paper I'm co-authoring. Over the summer I also worked as a casual summer intern at Manchester's School of Engineering, focusing on the digital side of a BrokenWire test and evaluation kit. Alongside these, I worked remotely as an app development intern at Travel Radar, building new features in sprints within an Agile team.

Next, I'm looking to keep expanding my knowledge and research, whether through a PhD, a master's or a graduate scheme. If you're hiring and able to offer visa sponsorship, feel free to get in touch.
